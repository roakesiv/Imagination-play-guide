Free activities for ages 4 and 6. Pick a website and choose something to print today.

Favorite shows

  • PBS KIDS for Parents Choose a favorite show, age or topic to find coloring sheets, mazes and bookmarks.
  • Bluey Make pretend-play props, learn to draw Bluey or print decorations for a birthday party.
  • Sesame Street Try Elmo’s picture-led activities for preschoolers practicing letters and numbers.
  • Daniel Tiger’s Neighborhood Color Daniel’s neighbors, fold a paper trolley or print bedtime routine cards.
  • Wild Kratts Make a creature-power suit or take an adventure passport on a wildlife hunt.
  • Gabby’s Dollhouse Choose Printables for a paper tambourine or dollhouse puzzle. A grown-up can help with cutting.
  • Peppa Pig Color Peppa’s family, join the dots or spot the differences between two pictures.
  • Spidey and His Amazing Friends Print Canon’s four-page comic kit (PDF) and draw your own Spidey adventure. Browse Canon’s Disney collection
  • PAW Patrol Print pup masks for pretend play, count dinosaurs or find a path through the Adventure Bay maze.
  • My Little Pony Try a pony matching game or make pegasus wings. Hasbro’s Share Your Sparkle! activities are for ages 4–6.

Puzzles, drawing & stories

  • Highlights Find a quiet-time Hidden Pictures puzzle, a drawing challenge or a scavenger hunt.
  • Play Nintendo Try a Yoshi maze, make Mario bookmarks or color Pokémon by number.
  • The Gruffalo and friends Follow a read-aloud with a search-and-find game or a Room on the Broom activity.
  • Crayola Let your child choose a coloring page by interest, from dinosaurs to unicorns.

Math & reading

  • The Math Learning Center Print a whole Kindergarten or Grade 1 math practice book, or just a few pages. Available in English and Spanish.
  • Numberblocks · BBC Learning Hub Play roll-and-color and counting games for ages 4–7. Downloads require a free account.
  • Florida Center for Reading Research Play Kindergarten and Grade 1 rhyming and sound games together. Some use cards to cut out.

Science, nature & building

  • Boston Children’s Museum Open the Tinker Kit Activity Sheets to build and test a paper bridge together.
  • NASA Space Place Pick the Moon, a planet or a Mars rover to color.
  • San Diego Zoo Wildlife Explorers Make an animal mask, color real wildlife or play a matching game.
  • National Park Service Junior Ranger Online Explore fossils, caves or the night sky with a themed booklet. Read a few pages together.
  • LEGO Print a building challenge and use bricks you already have. Can you build a way across a dragon’s moat?
  • California Academy of Sciences Choose the California Coast or Sharks pack labeled ages 4–8 for coloring, crafts and hands-on science.
  • Monterey Bay Aquarium Print two sets of kelp forest critter cards, color and cut them out, then play a matching game.
  • NASA’s Traveler Trace rockets, count stars or play a space memory game. These printables are designed for preschoolers.
  • NASA Jet Propulsion Laboratory Try building a tower with pasta or testing a parachute together. Look for the grade and time beside each activity.

Parent tutorial

Print something for today

  1. Open the printable books and choose an age to see its volumes. Expand Individual books and binder pages for a single character’s book.
  2. Select Open PDF. In the PDF viewer, choose Print. On a phone, look in the Share or menu options.
  3. Pick the pages you want and your printer. You can also use Download to keep the whole book for later.

Make a creature together

  1. Ask what animals make up your creature and what magic it can do. Type your child’s words into the maker.
  2. Select Make Creature for a summary and an image prompt. If you want a picture, copy the prompt into your image creator.
  3. Select Save Creature to keep the card in this browser. Export Saved Data downloads your current draft and saved cards as a JSON file. The maker currently supports export only.
