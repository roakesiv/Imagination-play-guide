Parent tutorial

Print something for today

  1. Open the printable books. Choose Volume 1 or Volume 2 for a collection, or scroll down to pick a single character’s book.
  2. Select Open PDF. In the PDF viewer, choose Print. On a phone, look in the Share or menu options.
  3. Pick the pages you want and your printer. You can also use Download to keep the whole book for later.

Make a creature together

  1. Ask what animals make up your creature and what magic it can do. Type your child’s words into the maker.
  2. Select Make Creature for a summary and an image prompt. If you want a picture, copy the prompt into your image creator.
  3. Select Save Creature to keep the card in this browser. Export Saved Data downloads your current draft and saved cards as a JSON file. The maker currently supports export only.
