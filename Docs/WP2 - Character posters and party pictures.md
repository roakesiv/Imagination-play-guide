# WP2 · Character posters and party pictures

The gallery offers two ready-to-print US Letter PDFs for each of the 14 published characters. Open a name and choose **Print this character** for one large picture or **Four party pictures** for a sheet to cut apart. Use Print in the PDF viewer or Share → Print on a phone. The documents contain the artwork alone, with cut guides on the four-picture sheets. [Character gallery](https://roakesiv.github.io/Imagination-play-guide/#characters)

**Posters & party pictures** also guides custom designs. Choose a Rhea character, a saved creature on this device, the current maker draft, or your own idea. The poster maker opens with **My child or my own idea** and the questions ready to fill in. A Rhea character appears in a prompt only when selected. To switch back from a character, choose **Use my child or my own idea instead** to keep the answers already entered. Six optional questions cover the occasion, favorite colors, setting, creatures, a reference picture, and other details. Choose the layout, age, color treatment, lettering, and number of versions. Add a shared mood or a direction for varying the images. Copy the finished prompt into an image creator and attach the personal reference there if selected. A Rhea subject also offers its reference illustration for download. Each requested version is a separate poster or a separate party sheet with four matching pictures. Printable PDFs carry the original colors. For a grayscale copy of an existing picture, select black and white in your printer settings. [Poster and party maker](https://roakesiv.github.io/Imagination-play-guide/#posters)
