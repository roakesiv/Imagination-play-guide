# WP2 · Character posters and party pictures

The gallery offers two ready-to-print US Letter PDFs for each of the 14 published characters. Open a name and choose **Print this character** for one large picture or **Four party pictures** for a sheet to cut apart. Use Print in the PDF viewer or Share → Print on a phone. The documents contain the artwork alone, with cut guides on the four-picture sheets. [Character gallery](https://roakesiv.github.io/Imagination-play-guide/#characters)

**Posters & party pictures** also guides custom designs. Choose a Rhea character, a saved creature on this device, the current maker draft, or your own idea. Choose a layout, age, color treatment, and optionally a theme and lettering. Make and copy the prompt into an image creator. A Rhea subject also offers its reference illustration for download. Printable PDFs carry the original colors. For a grayscale copy of an existing picture, select black and white in your printer settings. [Poster and party maker](https://roakesiv.github.io/Imagination-play-guide/#posters)
